Diagnostic & Laboratory Workups

Essential investigations performed or evaluated by Dr. Anil Kumar to formulate your precise treatment plan.

Serum Creatinine & eGFR

Quantifies the filtration rate of your kidneys and determines precise CKD staging to guide medical dosing safely.

Urine Albumin-to-Creatinine Ratio (uACR)

The earliest and most sensitive test for kidney damage, detecting microscopic protein leakage long before symptoms arise.

Renal Ultrasound & Doppler

Assesses kidney size, cortical thickness, urinary tract obstructions, kidney stones, and blood flow through the renal arteries.

Electrolyte & Acid-Base Panel

Monitors serum potassium, sodium, bicarbonate, and calcium-phosphorus balance crucial for cardiac and bone health.

What to Bring to Your Consultation

Help us review your health history thoroughly by preparing the following records:

Essential Medical Records

  • All previous blood tests (creatinine, urea, electrolytes, HbA1c, complete blood count)
  • Urine test reports (routine urine microscopy and protein/ACR reports)
  • Ultrasound KUB (Kidney, Ureter, Bladder) or CT/MRI abdomen scans and films
  • Recent discharge summaries or hospital admission records

Medications & BP History

  • Complete list of current prescription medicines with exact dosages and brand names
  • Over-the-counter painkillers, herbal medicines, or health supplements you consume
  • Home blood pressure log or blood glucose records from the past 2–4 weeks
  • List of any drug allergies or sensitivities
Emergency Symptoms: If you experience severe shortness of breath, sudden cessation of urine, acute chest pain, or high fever with severe back pain, do not wait for an outpatient appointment. Proceed directly to the nearest hospital casualty or call 108.

Kidney Stones (Nephrolithiasis)

Kidney stones are hard crystalline mineral deposits that crystallize inside the kidneys. While treating acute pain is vital, the core nephrological mission is uncovering the underlying metabolic causes to ensure you never suffer another agonizing episode.

Why Kidney Stones Form

Understanding crystallization chemistry and the four primary stone types.

The Mechanism of Stone Formation

Stones develop when urine becomes supersaturated with minerals like calcium, oxalate, phosphate, or uric acid, while lacking natural inhibitors such as citrate. Concentrated urine creates an ideal environment for microscopic crystals to precipitate, aggregate, and grow into stones.

As stones migrate from the kidney into the narrow ureter, they trigger intense muscle spasms and urinary obstruction (renal colic), leading to severe flank pain and microscopic or visible hematuria.

Primary Kidney Stone Compositions

Calcium Oxalate: Most prevalent stone; linked to low fluid intake, high dietary oxalate, or low calcium binding. ~75%
Uric Acid: Associated with high purine intake, metabolic syndrome, diabetes, or persistently acidic urine. ~10%
Struvite (Infection): Linked to chronic urease-producing urinary tract infections. ~10%
Cystine: Rare hereditary genetic condition (cystinuria). ~1%

Warning Signs of Kidney Stones

Classic clinical symptoms of an active or migrating stone:

  • Severe Flank & Back Pain: Sharp, excruciating pain in the side or lower back below the ribs that arrives in waves (colic).
  • Radiation to the Groin: Pain migrating downwards along the urinary tract into the lower abdomen, groin, or inner thigh.
  • Hematuria (Blood in Urine): Urine appearing visibly pink, reddish, or tea-colored, or discovered on routine microscopic urinalysis.
  • Urinary Urgency & Burning: Intense urge to urinate frequently with painful, burning sensations while voiding.
  • Nausea & Emesis: Profound nausea and repeated vomiting triggered by shared autonomic nerve pathways between the kidneys and gastrointestinal tract.

How Dr. Anil Kumar Helps

Comprehensive acute relief, medical therapy, and lifelong prevention:

  • Rapid Precision Diagnostics: Non-contrast CT KUB or high-resolution ultrasound to verify stone size, position, and urinary obstruction status.
  • Medical Expulsive Therapy (MET): Prescribing specialized alpha-blockers and hydration therapies to help pass stones under 5–6 mm naturally without surgery.
  • 24-Hour Urine Metabolic Workup: In-depth laboratory testing measuring urinary volume, pH, calcium, oxalate, uric acid, and citrate to pinpoint why your body produces stones.
  • Targeted Recurrence Prevention: Individualized dietary prescriptions (optimal water intake to yield 2.5L urine/day, dietary calcium balance, and sodium reduction).
  • Urologist Teamwork: Smooth coordination with experienced urologists if a large, impacted stone requires lithotripsy (ESWL) or laser ureteroscopy (URS).
Critical Red Flag Warning: A kidney stone causing urinary tract blockage combined with a urinary tract infection is a life-threatening medical emergency known as obstructive pyelonephritis. If stone pain is accompanied by fever, shaking chills, total inability to pass urine, or uncontrollable vomiting, do not wait. Rush immediately to hospital casualty or call 108.

Know the Warning Signs

Kidney disease often develops silently without pain. Seek medical evaluation if you experience any of these symptoms:

  • Unexplained swelling in feet, ankles, hands, or morning puffiness around the eyes
  • Persistent foamy or frothy urine, or pink/tea-coloured blood in urine
  • Needing to urinate significantly more often at night, or a sudden decrease in urine volume
  • Unexplained fatigue, weakness, loss of appetite, metallic taste, or severe dry itching
  • High blood pressure that is difficult to control or diagnosed at a young age
